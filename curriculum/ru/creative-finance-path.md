# Creative Finance Curriculum Notes

## Purpose

Guide the agent when teaching investor-style acquisition methods. The actual learner-facing teaching must be in Russian.

## Russian learner framing

Creative finance is not a magic shortcut. It is a set of advanced deal structures that may reduce some barriers but increase legal, title, insurance, financing, and default risks.

The agent must always teach both the opportunity and the downside.

## Lesson structure

Use this Russian-facing structure:

```markdown
## Стратегия
### Что это такое простыми словами
### Как инвесторы это используют
### Когда это может работать
### Почему это опасно
### Какие документы и специалисты нужны
### Какие вопросы задать
### Пример расчета
### Красные флаги
### Мини-задание
```

## Strategy modules

- seller financing;
- assumable financing;
- lease option;
- lease purchase;
- land contract concept;
- private money;
- hard money;
- DSCR-style investor financing;
- house hacking;
- renovation and refinance path;
- partnership structures;
- advanced existing-debt structures with enhanced warnings.

## Required learner outcomes

The learner should be able to:

- explain the difference between ownership, debt, possession, and option rights;
- identify why a seller may accept terms;
- understand that low cash upfront can hide larger risks;
- identify red flags;
- know which professionals must review the deal;
- compare classic and creative paths honestly;
- produce a go/no-go memo before paying money or signing.

## Required outputs

- creative finance strategy comparison;
- seller conversation intake;
- risk review;
- title and existing debt question list;
- payment flow summary;
- exit strategy memo;
- go/no-go decision memo.

## Safety rule

If a proposed structure depends on secrecy, misrepresentation, missing title review, or pressure to pay quickly, the agent must mark it as a red flag and recommend pausing until professional review is completed.
