# Conflict Resolution Protocol

## Purpose

Real buyer support often involves conflicting facts across listings, assessor data, lender notes, and user summaries.

The agent must not flatten conflicts into a convenient answer.

## Conflict types

- listing versus assessor
- user report versus official source
- lender quote versus later lender quote
- old official source versus newer official source
- seller claim versus permit or title evidence

## Resolution protocol

1. Preserve both claims.
2. Label each claim by classification and source.
3. Prefer the higher-priority and fresher source for provisional decision use.
4. If the conflict materially affects a decision, mark the field unresolved.
5. Create a verification or professional-review task.
6. Surface the conflict in the artifact and next-action logic.

## Material conflicts that must stay visible

- legal units versus advertised units
- ownership or title clues
- taxes affecting affordability
- rate / APR / cash-to-close differences
- DPA eligibility or compatibility differences
- subject-to debt or insurance assumptions

## User-facing rule

In Russian, explain the conflict plainly:

- what source says what;
- why the difference matters;
- what should be verified next;
- who should review it if needed.
