## Fit labels

Use one of:

- strong potential fit;
- possible fit;
- unlikely fit;
- not enough information;
- not applicable.

## Questions for lender

- Does this lender participate in the program?
- Can this program be combined with the user's likely loan type?
- Does it change the interest rate, fees, or closing timeline?
- Is the assistance a grant, forgivable loan, deferred loan, second mortgage, or tax-credit-style benefit?
- Are there repayment, recapture, resale, refinance, or occupancy rules?
- Is homebuyer education required?
- What documents are required?
- What deadlines apply?

## Questions for program administrator

- What are current income limits?
- What are current purchase price limits?
- Which cities, counties, census tracts, or neighborhoods are eligible?
- What property types are allowed?
- Does the buyer need to be a first-time buyer?
- Are non-citizens or different immigration statuses eligible, if relevant?
- Can the program be layered with seller credits?
- How long does approval usually take?

## Documents likely needed

- ID;
- income documentation;
- asset statements;
- pre-approval or lender contact;
- purchase contract if already under contract;
- homebuyer education certificate if required;
- tax returns if required;
- proof of household size if required.

## Red flags

- Program is marketed as guaranteed free money.
- Source is not official or reputable.
- Fees are requested before eligibility review.
- Repayment terms are unclear.
- Assistance is called a grant but functions like a loan.
- Timeline does not fit the planned closing.
- Lender does not participate.
