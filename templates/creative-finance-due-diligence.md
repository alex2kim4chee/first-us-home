## Questions to ask before moving forward

- What exactly does the user receive now: ownership, option, lease rights, or another right?
- Who is responsible for taxes, insurance, repairs, and utilities?
- What happens if the user cannot complete the exit plan?
- What happens if the seller cannot perform their obligations?
- What documents must be reviewed before money changes hands?
