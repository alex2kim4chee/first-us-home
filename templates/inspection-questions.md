# Inspection Questions Template

Draft for educational and organizational purposes only. Physical condition decisions should be reviewed with qualified inspectors and specialists.

## General inspection questions

- What are the biggest safety issues?
- What are the most expensive likely repairs?
- What needs immediate attention?
- What can wait?
- Are there signs of water intrusion?
- Are there foundation or structural concerns?
- What is the approximate age and condition of the roof?
- What is the condition of HVAC?
- What is the condition of plumbing?
- What is the condition of electrical systems?
- Are there signs of mold, pests, or moisture?
- Are there drainage or grading concerns?
