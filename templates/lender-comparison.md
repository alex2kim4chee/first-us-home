## Questions to ask lenders

- What loan program is this quote based on?
- What is the difference between the interest rate and APR here?
- Are points included?
- What fees are lender fees versus third-party fees?
- Is mortgage insurance included in the payment?
- What is the estimated cash to close?
- What documents are still needed?
- What could change before closing?
- Is the rate locked? If yes, until when?
- Are there state or local assistance programs that fit this buyer?
